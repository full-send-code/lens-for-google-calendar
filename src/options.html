info About Logs

Extension logs are stored locally on your device and are automatically anonymized (calendar IDs and email addresses are replaced with placeholders). Logs are retained for 24 hours and limited to a maximum of 500 entries. When you export logs, they are packaged as a ZIP file and automatically cleared after successful export. No data is sent to external servers.
