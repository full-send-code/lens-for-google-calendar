info About Logs

Extension logs are stored locally on your device and are automatically anonymized (calendar IDs and email addresses are replaced with placeholders). Logs are retained for 24 hours and limited to a maximum of 500 entries. When logging is enabled, you can download logs as a ZIP file for troubleshooting purposes. No data is sent to external servers.
